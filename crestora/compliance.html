The short version

What a security review usually wants to know, in six lines.

  • In place

    GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA, with a Data Processing Agreement already built into every plan.

  • Audited

    A SOC 2 Type II report covering a full year of controls, shared under NDA in two working days.

  • Certified

    ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for the whole company: people, offices and code, not just a data centre.

  • Listed

    Six subprocessors, named below, with 30 days’ notice and a right to object before anything changes.

  • Your region

    Label workspaces stay in the EU or the US, backups and support access included.

  • Accessible

    WCAG 2.2 AA is our bar for the product and this site, and we publish where we still fall short.

Data Processing Agreement

In shortIt already applies to every plan. Ask for a countersigned copy and you’ll have one in two working days.

Our Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is part of the Terms of service, so it covers you from the moment you open a lane, on every plan, free included. You don’t need to sign anything for it to apply. If your legal team wants a countersigned copy for its files, request one and we’ll send it for e-signature.

Data residency

In shortEvery workspace lives in one region. On Label, you pin it there, and everything stays.

Each workspace is created in one region and stays there. On the Label plan you can pin a workspace to the EU or the US, and then everything it holds stays in that region: files and versions, notes and approvals, search indexes, logs and backups.

EU

Ireland and GermanyPrimary storage in Ireland, encrypted backups in Germany. The default for Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

US

Oregon and VirginiaPrimary storage in Oregon, encrypted backups in Virginia. The default for the Americas and Asia-Pacific.

Support access to a pinned workspace is limited to named staff, needs your approval for each session, and is logged action by action. Changing region later is a guided migration, which takes about a day for each terabyte.

Accessibility statement

In shortWe aim for WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere, we test with disabled creators, and we list what isn’t there yet.

Crestora should work for everyone who makes things, however they see, hear, move or think. Our target for the apps and for this website is WCAG 2.2 level AA. We were last tested in October 2026, by an independent auditor and by three disabled creators who are paid for their time.

  • Everything works from a keyboard, with a visible focus, and with the screen readers built into desktop and mobile systems.
  • Review Room shows captions on video and transcribes audio notes.
  • Ask your system for reduced motion and Crestora, this site included, comes to rest: no intros, no reveals, nothing moving on its own.

Where we fall short

  • Waveform notes on audio (new in v4.12) can’t yet be fully reviewed with a screen reader. A text timeline for them ships in v4.13.
  • Nine of the 40 Blueprint templates still show status by colour alone. We’re fixing them one by one.

If something stands in your way, write to access@crestora.example. We reply within two working days, and we’ll find a way to get your work through while we fix it.
