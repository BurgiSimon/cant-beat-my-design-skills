    Getting started

    Your first lane, your first crew, and the work you already have.

    4 answers
    How do I open my first lane?

    Press N anywhere in Crestora, or choose New lane at the top of the sidebar. Give it a name, say what you’re making, and pick a Blueprint template or start blank. Every lane starts in Spark, so drop in whatever you have: a voice memo, a screenshot, one good sentence.

    On Solo you can keep three lanes active at once, free, for as long as you like.

    Your first lane in four steps

    What’s the difference between a lane and a project?

    A lane is a project with a road under it. It holds everything for one thing you’ll launch (the brief, the files, the notes and the release) and moves through six stages as the work does.

    One lane per launch is a good rule of thumb: a short film is a lane, and its trailer can be another.

    How lanes work

    How do I invite people to a lane?

    Open the lane, choose Share, then Invite. Add email addresses and pick a role for each person: Maker for the people who make, Reviewer for the people who give notes. They get an email with a sign-in link, with no password to set.

    Reviewers don’t even need that: send a review link and they can leave notes straight from their browser.

    What each role can do

    Can I bring in work from other tools?

    Yes. Drag a folder onto a lane and Crestora keeps its structure, with each file as a first version. Tasks come in from a CSV, and connected apps send new work straight to the right stage, so renders from Framewright land in Make as versions.

    Importing, in detail

    Account & billing

    Plans, invoices, lane limits and taking everything with you.

    5 answers
    How do I change or cancel my plan?

    Go to Settings, then Plan. Upgrades start right away, and you pay only the difference for the rest of the period. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the period you’ve paid for, and nothing is deleted when they do.

    No call, no form, no retention offer.

    Compare every plan

    What counts as an active lane?

    Any lane that isn’t archived, whatever stage it’s in. Solo includes three; Studio and Crew have no limit.

    When a project has launched and its Afterglow recaps are in, archive it. It stays readable, searchable and exportable, and stops counting toward your three.

    Archiving and deleting

    Where do I find invoices and change billing details?

    In Settings, then Billing. Every invoice is there as a PDF, and you can add a VAT or tax ID, a company name and an address; changes apply from the next invoice.

    Every plan can pay by card. Label can also pay by bank transfer against an annual invoice.

    How do I export everything?

    Go to Settings, then Export. Pick a few lanes or take the whole workspace: files at full quality, every version, notes as JSON and CSV, and recaps as PDFs. We email you a download link when it’s ready, usually within the hour.

    It’s free, on every plan, as often as you like. Your work is yours.

    What an export contains

    Is there a discount for students or first-time founders?

    Better than a discount: students, educators and first-time founders can have Studio free for a year through a Creator grant. Applying takes about five minutes, and we decide within two weeks.

    How Creator grants work

    Lanes & stages

    The six stages, moving back, merging and archiving.

    4 answers
    What are the six stages?

    Every lane moves through the same six, and each one opens its own tool.

    1. 01SparkSparkboard catches the idea before it fades.
    2. 02ShapeBlueprints turns it into a one-page plan.
    3. 03MakeWorkbench keeps every version and what changed.
    4. 04ReviewReview Room holds the notes and the approvals.
    5. 05LaunchCountdown runs the checklist and the schedule.
    6. 06AfterglowAfterglow recaps how it landed on day 1, 7 and 30.

    The stages, in depth

    Can a lane move backwards?

    Yes, and often it should. Drag the lane chip to any earlier stage, or press ⇧ ←. Versions, notes and approvals travel with it, and the lane’s history records every move.

    A second round of Review is never a step back. It’s just another stop.

    Moving a lane

    How do I merge two lanes?

    From the lane menu choose Merge into, then pick the lane to keep. Files, versions and notes come across in date order, and anything with the same name becomes a new version rather than a duplicate.

    Merging arrived in v4.10, and a merge can be undone for seven days.

    Read the v4.10 notes

    What happens when I archive or delete a lane?

    Archiving makes a lane read-only and frees its slot; you can restore it any time. Deleting moves it to Trash for 30 days. After that it leaves our live systems within 24 hours and our backups within 30 days.

    If in doubt, archive.

    How we handle your work

    Review & feedback

    Review links, pinned notes and approvals that stick.

    4 answers
    Do reviewers need an account?

    No. Share a review link and anyone who has it can watch, read or listen in their browser, type their name and pin notes. You can give a link an expiry date and a password, and revoke it in one click.

    Reviewers are always free, on every plan.

    Review links

    Can I pin a note to a frame or a second of audio?

    Yes. Notes pin to a frame of video, a pixel of an image, a spot on a PDF page, a line of code and, since v4.12, a moment on an audio waveform. Press C to comment at the playhead.

    Every note keeps its timecode, so the editor lands on the exact frame you meant.

    Every kind of note

    How do approvals work?

    Choose Ask for approval on a version and pick who signs off. Each approver gets a clear Approve or Request changes, and every decision is recorded with their name, the time and the exact version.

    On Crew, approvals also go into an audit trail you can export for a client or a contract.

    Approvals

    Where do resolved notes go?

    They fold into the version they were left on, so the conversation stays with the work. Switch on Show resolved to see them again.

    When you upload a new version, Crestora offers to carry the open notes across and leaves the resolved ones behind.

    Launching

    Checklists, channels, launch rooms and moving the date.

    4 answers
    Where does the launch checklist come from?

    Countdown builds it from your Blueprint. Every milestone becomes a step, and each connected channel adds its own: a newsletter to write, a store page to fill, a build to submit.

    Assign steps, add your own, or tick them off as they happen. It fills itself in; you just check it.

    Checklists

    Which channels can Countdown publish to?

    Studio and up schedule to 14 channels, from newsletters and online stores to social scheduling, live streams and app-store builds, through apps like Postbox, Shopfront and Clipdeck. Solo includes one channel.

    Every post sits in one schedule you can read at a glance, and moves if the date does.

    Launch channels

    What is a launch room?

    A live page for launch day. The crew sees the countdown, the checklist, a chat and the first numbers as they come in, all in one place.

    Guests can join by link, so your sister, your editor or your beta testers can watch it go live with you.

    Launch rooms

    I need to move the launch date. What happens?

    Drag the date on the lane’s calendar, or change it in Countdown. Every scheduled post moves with it and keeps its spacing, and the crew gets one note about the change.

    Anything already published stays published: Countdown never recalls a post.

    Integrations

    Connecting your tools, and what they’re allowed to see.

    4 answers
    How do I connect an app?

    Open a lane, choose Integrations, pick the app and sign in to it. Then say what should flow in and to which stage, such as renders to Make or orders to Afterglow.

    It takes about 30 seconds, and the same app can feed as many lanes as you like.

    Browse 120+ integrations

    What can a connected app see?

    Only what you allow. Every app asks for the narrowest access it needs, shown before you connect, and most are read-only.

    You can see what each one has sent, and revoke it at any time, from Settings, then Integrations.

    Integration permissions

    The app I use isn’t listed. Now what?

    Tell us. Requests go straight to the integrations team, and the most-asked ones ship first.

    If you can’t wait, the API lets you send versions, notes and stats from anything that can make a web request.

    Request an integrationAPI reference

    Why hasn’t my render shown up yet?

    Most work arrives within a minute. Large files upload in the background and appear when they’re complete, with a progress chip on the lane.

    If nothing has arrived after ten minutes, open Integrations, then Activity. An expired sign-in is the usual cause, and reconnecting fixes it.

    Crews & roles

    Roles, seats, handing a lane over and portals for clients.

    4 answers
    What can each role do?
    Owner
    Everything, including billing, roles and deleting the lane.
    Maker
    Adds work, moves stages, runs reviews and launches.
    Reviewer
    Watches, reads and listens; leaves notes and approves.
    Guest
    Sees what you share with them, and nothing more.

    The full permissions table

    Who needs a paid seat?

    On Crew, Owners and Makers have seats. Reviewers and guests are always free, however many you invite.

    Crew starts at two seats, at $16 a seat a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly.

    The Crew plan

    How do I hand a lane over to someone else?

    Open the lane’s settings and choose Transfer ownership. The new owner accepts by email, and the lane moves with its full history.

    You stay on as a Maker unless you choose to leave.

    Can a client see our work without seeing everything else?

    Yes, with client review portals on Crew. A client gets a page with your logo and only the versions you send them, where they can leave notes and approve.

    They never see other lanes, internal notes or who else is on the crew.

    Troubleshooting

    Sign-in links, stuck uploads, iOS sync and the status page.

    4 answers
    My sign-in link didn’t arrive.

    Crestora signs you in with an emailed link, never a password. Links can take a minute to arrive, and they expire after 15 minutes. Check spam and any Promotions tab, then ask for a new one.

    If your company filters mail, add signin@crestora.example to your safe senders.

    Get a new sign-in link

    My upload is stuck.

    Uploads pick up where they stopped if your connection drops, so a pause usually fixes itself. If one sits at the same percentage for more than five minutes, pause and resume it from the upload tray, or try another browser.

    Single files can be up to 50 GB on Studio and Crew.

    Supported files

    Sparkboard on iOS hasn’t synced my captures.

    Sparkboard works offline on iOS and syncs when you’re back on a connection. Open the app with a signal for a few seconds and the captures go up in the order you made them.

    Nothing leaves your phone until our servers confirm they have it, so a capture can wait, but it can’t get lost.

    Offline Sparkboard, in v4.8

    Is Crestora down, or is it me?

    The status page knows, and the on-call crew keeps it current: every tool, the last 90 days, and anything happening right now.

    If it says all is well and something still isn’t, write to Support with the lane’s name and what you expected to happen.

    Check the status page
