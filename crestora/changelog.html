Releases

8 releases · 30 changes

v4.134 Dec 2026

Up next. Shared Sparkboards go to early access, and Android opens its beta. See the roadmap

  1. v4.12 20 Nov 2026 Review Room
    NewImprovedFixed

    Review Room hears you

    Pin a note to a second of audio the way you pin one to a frame. Mixes, podcast episodes and voiceovers now open as a waveform in Review Room: click anywhere on it to leave a timestamped note, and your reviewer hears the exact moment you mean, looped with half a second either side.

    Also in v4.12

    • ImprovedNotes on video show the frame number and the timecode side by side.
    • FixedApproving a version from the email digest no longer asks you to sign in twice.
    • FixedLong reviewer names no longer push the Approve button off small screens.
  2. v4.11 6 Nov 2026 Afterglow
    NewImprovedFixed

    Afterglow recaps on day 1, 7 and 30

    Every launch now gets three recaps without you asking: the first night, the first week and the first month. Views, sales, replies and reviews from every connected channel sit on one page, the comments worth reading float to the top, and the ideas they spark go straight back to your Sparkboard.

    Also in v4.11

    • ImprovedShare a recap with your crew or a client as a read-only page.
    • FixedSales from two storefronts on the same day are no longer counted twice.
    • FixedThe day 1 recap now runs on the launch’s time zone, not the viewer’s.
  3. v4.10 23 Oct 2026 Workbench
    NewImprovedFixed

    Lanes can merge

    Two lanes that turned out to be one project can now become one. Merge them and Crestora keeps both version histories side by side, folds the reviews together and asks you which plan wins, so nothing gets left at the side of the road.

    Also in v4.10

    • ImprovedCompare works across lanes, not just inside one.
    • FixedA version dragged to another lane now brings its new thumbnail with it.
    • FixedRestoring an archived lane brings back its pinned notes too.
  4. v4.9 9 Oct 2026 Countdown
    NewImprovedFixed

    Countdown launch rooms

    Launch night has a room now. Open it from any Countdown and the whole crew sees the checklist, the schedule for every channel and the last seconds ticking down together, then watches the first replies come in, live, in one place instead of six group chats.

    Also in v4.9

    • ImprovedCountdown schedules to 14 channels, up from 9.
    • FixedChecklist items moved between sections keep their owner.
    • FixedThe countdown no longer drifts a second behind on phones left asleep.
  5. v4.8 25 Sep 2026 Sparkboard
    ImprovedFixed

    Sparkboard works offline on iOS

    Capture on the train; it syncs when you surface. Voice memos, photos and notes caught without signal wait safely on your phone, keep the time and place you caught them, and land on the right board the moment you’re back online.

    Also in v4.8

    • ImprovedThe share sheet remembers the last board you sent something to.
    • FixedVoice memos longer than ten minutes upload in one piece.
    • FixedSparks caught just after midnight land on the right day.
  6. v4.7 11 Sep 2026 Blueprints
    NewFixed

    40 Blueprint templates from working creators

    Start your plan from one that already shipped. Forty Blueprint templates, each written by someone who uses it: a short-film schedule from an animator, a beta plan from an indie developer, a spring drop from a potter. Pick one and its milestones land on your calendar, ready to bend.

    Also in v4.7

    • FixedMilestones on the last day of a month no longer slip into the next one.
    • FixedDuplicating a Blueprint keeps its crew, not just its pages.
  7. v4.6 28 Aug 2026 Workbench
    ImprovedFixed

    Lanes open 2.3× faster

    We rebuilt how a lane loads. Big ones, with hundreds of versions and thousands of notes, now open in under a second on an ordinary laptop: 2.3 times faster than before, and easier on your battery while they’re open.

    Also in v4.6

    • ImprovedThumbnails for long videos are made in the background, so uploads finish sooner.
    • FixedScrolling a long version history no longer jumps back to the top.
    • FixedSearch finds notes on archived versions again.
  8. v4.5 14 Aug 2026 Review Room
    NewImprovedFixed

    Review links without accounts

    Reviewers need a link, not an account. Send one to a client, a sister or a festival programmer and they can watch, listen, pin notes and approve without signing up for anything. You choose how long the link lives and whether it asks for a password.

    Also in v4.5

    • ImprovedOn Crew and Label, guests see your logo and lane name, not ours.
    • FixedReview links opened inside a chat app’s browser keep the reviewer’s name.
