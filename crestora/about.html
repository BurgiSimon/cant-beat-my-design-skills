About us

We build roads for people who make things.

Crestora started as a fix for three friends who kept losing their projects between tools. Five years on, 64 of us build it for more than 38,000 creators, builders and dreamers.

Read how it started Meet the crew

Our story

It started with a lost thread.

In the winter of 2021, in a shared studio above a bakery in Lisbon, three friends kept losing their work.

Mira Castell was cutting a documentary. Jonah Feld was writing code for anyone who would pay him. Ama Boateng was firing a kiln twice a week and selling what came out of it online. Between them they used fifteen tools, and every project fell through the gaps: the brief in one, the files in another, the feedback in a chat nobody could find on launch day.

So they drew a road on the studio wall. Six lanes, from the first spark to the night it goes out, and a magnet for every project that showed where it really was. Their first lane was Mira’s documentary, Salt Roads. It premiered in Porto eleven weeks later, on the day she said it would.

Friends asked for the wall. Then friends of friends. By the summer, the wall was software, and the three of them were a company.

Our mission

Make the distance between an idea and its audience as short as a night drive.

What we hold to

Four rules for the road.

We wrote these on the studio wall, next to the first lane. They still settle most arguments.

  1. 01

    Makers first

    We build for the person with the idea, not the person with the org chart. Every feature spends a week in a real maker’s lane before it ships.

  2. 02

    Clear roads

    Say what’s next, who has it and when it lands. If a plan or a pull request needs a meeting to explain it, it isn’t finished yet.

  3. 03

    Ship, then shine

    Small and real beats big and someday. We put things in people’s hands early, then polish the parts they actually use.

  4. 04

    Leave the light on

    Be kind, especially at 2 a.m. Launch nights run long and somebody is always further from home than you. Answer the message.
