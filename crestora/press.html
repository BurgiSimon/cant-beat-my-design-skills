Boilerplate

Words you can paste.

Two lengths, both checked and current. Copy either one as it is; we’d rather you didn’t trim the middle.

Short 50 words

Crestora is the creative workspace that carries a project from first spark to launch day, then shows how it landed. Each project rides a single lane through six stages, from Sparkboard to Afterglow. Founded in Lisbon in 2021, Crestora serves more than 38,000 creators, builders and dreamers around the world.

Long 120 words

Crestora is the creative workspace that carries a project from first spark to launch day, then shows how it landed. Every project rides a lane through six stages, each with its own tool: Sparkboard for catching ideas, Blueprints for one-page plans, Workbench for versions, Review Room for frame-accurate feedback, Countdown for launches across 14 channels, and Afterglow for recaps on day 1, 7 and 30. Reviewers need a link, not an account, and creators can export everything, always. Founded in Lisbon in early 2021 by film editor Mira Castell, engineer Jonah Feld and ceramicist Ama Boateng, Crestora has carried more than 1.2 million launches for 38,000 creators. Its 64 people work from four hubs in Lisbon, Toronto, Seoul and Nairobi.

Logo

The C and the name.

A chrome C, open to the right like a road bending out of sight, and the name in Michroma. Use the lockup where there’s room and the C alone where there isn’t.

  • Clear space Half the C’s height on every side.
  • Smallest lockup 96 px wide on screen, 24 mm in print.
  • Smallest C 16 px. Below that, write the name.
  • Files SVG, outlined, built in your browser.

Logo downloads are built in your browser and need JavaScript. Write to press@crestora.example and we’ll send the files.

Three don’ts

  • Don’t recolour the C. It’s chrome on dark and black on light. The stage colours mean stages, never the brand.

  • Don’t stretch it. Scale the C and the lockup evenly, from a corner, every time.

  • Don’t set it on busy images. Give it a calm, dark field, or a white one, and the clear space it needs.

Colour

Six stages, one night.

Every colour means a stage of the lane, always. Set them as light on black: hairlines, dots and soft glows, never big flat fills. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

The whole flow. All six together, in stage order, mean the whole road: a launch, the final call to action, the reading line at the top of the page. One word of spectrum ink per screen, at most.

Type

Two typefaces, two jobs.

Michroma is the road: wide, technical, for display and numerals. Inter is the people: everything you read, click or quote.

Michroma

Display and numerals

  • Headlines of six words or fewer, in sentence case
  • Stage and plan names, prices, counters, version numbers
  • Never for paragraphs, and never as small coloured text

Founders

The three of them.

All three take interviews. Portraits are available on request, shot in the Lisbon studio where the first lane was drawn.

  • MC

    Mira Castell

    CEO & co-founder

    A film editor for twelve years before Crestora, Mira cut two feature documentaries and more commercials than she’ll admit to. She runs the company the way she ran an edit: one timeline, clear notes, and ship on the day you said. She still edits one short film a year, in Crestora.

    Talks aboutCreative tools · Running a remote company · Documentary

  • JF

    Jonah Feld

    CTO & co-founder

    Jonah spent a decade freelancing as an engineer for studios, labels and one very demanding bakery. He leads engineering across the four hubs and still reviews changes to the sync engine himself. On weekends he builds a modular synthesizer, one module a month, and he says none of them are finished.

    Talks aboutOffline sync · Large creative files · Small engineering teams

  • AB

    Ama Boateng

    Product & co-founder

    Ama is a ceramicist who sold her work online long before she designed software. She leads product and design, and tests every feature on her own spring drop before it ships to anyone else. Her studio is two streets from the Lisbon hub, and her kiln is booked for March.

    Talks aboutDesigning for non-managers · Craft businesses · Launching small
