Featured lesson

Run a review that lands.

Twelve minutes with Amara Okafor and Hye‑jin Park on getting notes from a client that you can actually use. Pick a chapter to move the playhead.

Review Intermediate Video · 12 min

Running a review with clients

  • AOAmara Okafor Animator, Lagos
  • HPHye-jin Park Design lead, Review Room

Chapters

In the library

Transcript from 03:40

  1. Hye-jin So Ruth, your commissioning editor, opens the link. No account, no password. What does she see first?

  2. Amara The cut, and one line from me: notes on timing only, colour isn’t final. If you don’t say what you need, you get notes on everything.

  3. Amara Then she pauses, presses N and clicks right where it hurts. Frame 118: the petals close too early. It reads like the flower gives up.

  4. Hye-jin And when you upload v8, where does that note go?

  5. Amara It rides along with the frame. I re-cut, resolve it in v8, and Ruth sees it closed without either of us writing an email.
