How it flows

Six lanes from first spark to launch night.

Every project in Crestora rides one lane. As it moves, the right tools light up, and nothing gets left at the last stop.

  1. 01SparkSparkboard

    Catch it before it fades.

    Voice memos, screenshots, links and 2 a.m. sketches land on one board. Crestora groups them into constellations, so the good ones find each other.

    • Capture from phone, browser or email
    • Auto-grouped constellations
    • Promote any spark to a lane

  2. 02ShapeBlueprints

    Give it a shape you can share.

    Turn a spark into a one-page plan: what it is, who’s riding along, what done looks like and when the lights go green.

    • 40 templates by working creators
    • Milestones that know your calendar
    • Share a brief with one link

  3. 03MakeWorkbench

    Make it without losing the thread.

    Drop in a render, a build or a mix. Crestora keeps every version on the lane and tells you what changed.

    • Unlimited version history
    • Side-by-side compare
    • 120+ tools connected

  4. 04ReviewReview Room

    Feedback that doesn’t stall the engine.

    Pin a note to a frame, a pixel, a second of audio or a line of code. Reviewers need a link, not an account.

    • Frame- and pixel-accurate notes
    • Review links without sign-up
    • One-click approvals, on the record

  5. 05LaunchCountdown

    Ship on the day you said.

    A launch checklist built from your plan, a schedule for every channel and a launch room where the crew watches it go live together.

    • Checklists that fill themselves
    • Schedule to 14 channels
    • A live launch room with a countdown

  6. 06AfterglowAfterglow

    See how it landed. Start the next one.

    Views, sales, replies and reviews from every channel in one recap on day 1, 7 and 30. The ideas it sparked go straight back to your board.

    • Day 1 / 7 / 30 recaps
    • Every channel in one view
    • Next ideas back to the Sparkboard
