Questions

Asked, and answered.

The eight we hear most. Anything else goes to a human who knows billing inside out.

Ask about billing Browse the Help Center
Can I cancel any time?

Yes, in two clicks from Settings → Billing. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you’re in; yearly plans run to the end of the year you paid for. After that your lanes move to Solo, and nothing is deleted.

What counts as an active lane?

A lane that hasn’t launched or been archived. Once a lane reaches Launch, or you archive it, it stops counting and stays readable for good. So Solo is three projects in motion at once, not three projects ever.

Do reviewers pay?

Never. Anyone you send a review link to can watch, listen, pin notes and approve without an account or a seat. Seats are for the people who make the work; reviewers and guests are free on every plan.

What happens to my files if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. If you’re over Solo’s 5 GB or three active lanes, the extra lanes turn read-only: you can still open, review and export everything, and you choose which three keep moving. Upgrade again and they pick up where they stopped.

Do you give refunds?

Yes. Within 14 days of any charge, no questions asked; after that, yearly plans are refunded pro rata if Crestora hasn’t worked out for you. Write to billing@crestora.example and a human will sort it the same week.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Prices are set in US dollars, and we also bill in euros, pounds, Canadian dollars, Korean won and Kenyan shillings at a fixed local price, so your bill never moves with the exchange rate. Cards and local bank transfers both work, and Crew and Label can pay by invoice.

Is there a nonprofit discount?

Yes: 50% off Studio and Crew for registered nonprofits and community arts groups. Students, educators and first-time founders can have Studio free for a year through a creator grant.

Who owns my work?

You do, always. You give us a limited licence to store and show your work only the way you ask: to your crew, your reviewers and your Share Pages. We never sell it or train models on it, and you can export everything, any time, in the formats you brought it in. The details are in our Terms of Service.
