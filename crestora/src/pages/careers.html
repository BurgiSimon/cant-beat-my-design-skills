How we work

Remote-first, never remote-only.

We work from 19 countries and meet in four hubs. The rules are few, and they’re written down.

  • 19 countries

    Remote-first

    Every decision lives in writing, in a lane anyone can find. Nobody has to be in the room for it to count.

  • 4 hubs

    Four hubs

    Lisbon, Toronto, Seoul and Nairobi: desks, good coffee and a crit wall whenever you want company. Getting to your nearest one is on us.

  • 4 core hours

    Overlap, not office hours

    Each team picks four shared hours a day, so nobody calls in at midnight. The rest of the day is yours to arrange.

  • 12 Fridays a year

    Maker Fridays

    One Friday a month is for your own project. Finish the song, fix the bike, print the zine. Show it at the crit if you like.

Crestora is a fictional company; people, roles, pay bands and figures are illustrative.

How we hire

Four stops, no trick questions.

The whole road usually takes three to four weeks. You’ll know where you are on it at every stop.

We reply to every application within 10 days.

Week 1Week 2Week 3Week 4
  1. 01

    Chat

    30 minutes · week 1

    A video call with someone from the team you’d join. We talk about your work and ours, and you can ask anything, the band included.

  2. 02

    Craft conversation

    90 minutes · week 2

    Walk us through something you made. No whiteboard puzzles: we want to hear how you decide, not watch you perform.

  3. 03

    Paid trial day

    1 day, paid · week 3

    A real, scoped problem with the people you’d work with, on a day that suits you. We pay it at the top of the band.

  4. 04

    Meet the crew

    2–3 short calls · weeks 3–4

    Meet the people you’d ride with, one of the founders among them. Then we decide quickly, and tell you why either way.
