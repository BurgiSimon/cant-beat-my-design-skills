The short version

Six things worth knowing. The full policy below is what counts.

  • Yours

    Your work is yours. Every file, version, note and recap in a lane belongs to you, not to us.

  • What we need

    We collect what we need to run Crestora: your account, the work you put in it, and how the service is holding up.

  • Never

    We never sell data and we never train AI models on your files, ours or anyone else’s.

  • Your call

    Export or delete everything, any time, from Settings. No ticket, no waiting, no fee.

  • Told first

    Changes that matter reach you 30 days early, by email and in the app, before they apply.

  • Ask us

    Questions go to privacy@crestora.example, read by a human who can actually fix things.

Who we are

In shortCrestora, Lda. in Lisbon decides what happens to your account data. For personal data you put into your lanes, you decide and we follow.

Crestora, Lda. (“Crestora”, “we”, “us”) makes the Crestora workspace: the web app, the iOS and desktop apps, review links, Share Pages, our API and this website. We’re a company registered in Portugal, at Travessa do Farol Velho 12, 2º, 1200-417 Lisboa.

For the personal data we need to run your account (who you are, what you pay, how you use the service) we are the controller: we decide why and how it’s processed, and we answer for it.

Your lanes often hold personal data about other people too: the names in a cast list, the faces in a render, the email addresses of your client’s team. For that data, you (or the studio you work for) are the controller, and we process it only on your instructions, as a processor, under our Data Processing Agreement.

This policy doesn’t cover the apps you connect to a lane. Each integration has its own policy, and we show you what it can see before you connect it.

How we use it

In shortTo run Crestora, keep it safe, help you when you ask, make it better and keep the books. Not for ads, and not to train models.

  • To run Crestora. Store your lanes and sync them between devices, send review links, schedule your launches and build your Afterglow recaps on day 1, 7 and 30.
  • To keep it safe. Spot abuse and spam, block suspicious sign-ins, and investigate when something goes wrong.
  • To help you. Answer when you write. If fixing your problem means looking inside a lane, we ask first, look only as long as it takes, and log that we did.
  • To make it better. Learn which tools get used and where people get stuck, from usage data that is aggregated or has names removed. Analytics cookies in the app only run with your consent; see the Cookie policy.
  • To tell you things. Service emails you can’t turn off (security, billing, changes like this one) and product news you can, in one click, from any of them.
  • To meet the law. Keep tax records and answer lawful requests, as described below.

We don’t sell or rent personal data. We don’t build ad profiles. We don’t train AI models on your work, and we don’t let anyone else do it either.

Not in this version of the policy, and not in a future one.

Sharing & subprocessors

In shortWith the people you invite, a short list of companies that help us run Crestora, the tools you connect, and the law when it truly must. Nobody else.

We share personal data in five situations, and only these:

  • With the people you invite. Crew members, reviewers and guests see what their role in a lane allows, and nothing more.
  • With our subprocessors. A handful of companies help us run Crestora: cloud infrastructure, email delivery, payments, error monitoring and support tooling. Each signs a data processing agreement at least as strict as ours. The full list, with what each one does and where, is on our Compliance page, and we give 30 days’ notice before adding a new one.
  • With integrations you connect. Only the data an integration needs, and only after you approve the permissions it asks for. Disconnect it and the access ends at once.
  • When the law requires it. We push back on requests that are vague or too broad, and we tell you before we hand anything over, unless a court forbids it.
  • If Crestora changes hands. Whoever owns Crestora next is bound by this policy, and you’ll hear from us before anything moves.

International transfers

In shortYour workspace lives in the EU or the US. When data has to cross a border, standard contract clauses and encryption go with it.

Every workspace lives in one region: the EU (Ireland and Germany) for workspaces opened in Europe, Africa and the Middle East, or the US (Oregon and Virginia) for the Americas and Asia-Pacific. Backups stay in the same region as the workspace.

Our crew works from four hubs, in Lisbon, Toronto, Seoul and Nairobi, so a support answer can come from outside your region. When personal data leaves the EEA or the UK, we protect it with the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum to them, together with encryption, access logging and need-to-know access.

On the Label plan you can pin everything to one region, support access included. See data residency.

Your rights and how to use them

In shortSee it, fix it, take it, delete it, or tell us to stop. Most of these are buttons in Settings, and none of them cost anything.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you, and what we do with it.
  • Correct anything that’s wrong or out of date.
  • Hand it over in a format another service can read: every file, version, note and recap.
  • Delete it, unless the law makes us keep something (like an invoice).
  • Stop or limit a use of it, including anything based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent you gave, for analytics or newsletters, as easily as you gave it.

If you’re in California, you also have the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We don’t sell or share it, so there’s nothing to opt out of, and we’ll never treat you differently for using any of these rights.

  1. Try Settings first. Settings → Account → Export everything and Close account work instantly, on every plan.
  2. Or write to us. Email privacy@crestora.example from the address on your account, or tell us how to confirm it’s you.
  3. Hear back within a week. We finish within 30 days, and tell you if a complicated request needs longer. It’s always free.

If you think we’ve got it wrong, please tell us first. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or to ours, in Portugal.

Children

In shortCrestora is for people 16 and over. Classrooms use it through their school, under a separate agreement.

Crestora isn’t meant for children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal data. Schools and colleges that use Crestora with younger students do so through our Education partners program, under an agreement that makes the school the controller and keeps students’ work private to their class.

If you believe a child under 16 has given us personal data, write to privacy@crestora.example and we’ll delete it.

Security

In shortEncrypted at rest and in transit, backed up four times a day, and if something goes wrong, you hear it from us within 72 hours.

Your work is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3, backed up every 6 hours to a second region, and protected by two-factor sign-in, passkeys and access that is time-boxed and logged. Our Security page has the details, and live status.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects your personal data, we’ll tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours of finding it: what happened, what it touched, what we’ve done and what you can do.
