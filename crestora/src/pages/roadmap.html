Now

Building now

3
  1. Every stageOpen beta in December

    Android app (beta)

    Capture on the bus, answer a review in the queue, watch your launch go live. Everything the iOS app does, on the phone most of the world actually carries.

    Added 20 Apr 2026
  2. ReviewPrivate beta, 40 studios

    Review Room for 3D files

    Pin a note to a point on a model, orbit it together with your reviewer, and compare two versions under the same light. For product designers, game artists and anyone whose work has a back side.

    Added 12 Jun 2026
  3. SparkEarly access on 4 Dec

    Shared Sparkboards

    One board for the whole crew. Everyone’s sparks land in the same constellations, with who caught what and when, so the best idea in the room doesn’t stay on one person’s phone.

    Added 3 Aug 2026

Nothing for this stage in Now. It may be further down the road.

Next

H1 2027

3
  1. LaunchDesigning

    Lane automations

    Small rules that keep work moving while you sleep: when a version is approved, move to Launch; when a launch goes live, start the recap; when a review stalls for two days, nudge.

    Added 15 Sep 2026
  2. ShapePrototyping

    Budgets in Blueprints

    Put a number next to the plan. Track spend against each milestone, so the money and the deadline ride in the same lane and nobody finds out at the end.

    Added 8 Jul 2026
  3. LaunchDesigning

    Share Pages in 12 languages

    Write your launch page once and publish it in twelve languages, with every translation reviewable line by line in Review Room before it goes live.

    Added 21 Oct 2026

Nothing for this stage in Next yet.

Later

On the horizon

3
  1. MakeExploring

    Bring-your-own storage

    Keep your files in your own storage, in the EU or the US, while Crestora handles the versions, notes and approvals on top. For studios with rules about where work lives.

    Added 27 May 2026
  2. MakeExploring

    Desktop offline mode

    Keep working at 30,000 feet. Versions and notes queue up on your laptop and sync when you land, the way Sparkboard already does on iOS.

    Added 30 Sep 2026
  3. ShapeTalking to teachers

    Crestora for classrooms

    Teachers set briefs as Blueprints, every student rides their own lane, and crits happen in Review Room. Built with the three art schools already using Crestora this way.

    Added 12 Nov 2026
