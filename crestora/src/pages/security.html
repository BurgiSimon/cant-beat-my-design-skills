Your work stays yours

Everything you put in a lane, from the first voice memo to the final master, belongs to you. We store it, process it and show it only in the ways you ask us to, and only to the people you invite.

  • Never used to train models We don’t train AI models on your files, notes or recaps, and we don’t let anyone else do it either.
  • Export any time Every file, version, note and recap, in open formats, from Settings. No ticket, no waiting, no fee.
  • Delete means delete Deleted lanes leave our live systems within 24 hours and our backups within 30 days.

Data protection

Your work is encrypted from the moment it leaves your device until an invited reviewer opens it, and copied somewhere safe four times a day.

At rest
AES-256On every file, version and note, with keys held in a dedicated key service and rotated every year.
In transit
TLS 1.3On every connection, with HSTS and modern ciphers only. Older protocols are refused.
Backups
Every 6 hoursEncrypted copies, kept for 30 days in a second region, then deleted for good.

Infrastructure

Crestora runs in two regions on an established cloud provider whose data centres hold ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 certifications. Your workspace lives in one region; on Label, you choose which.

EU

Ireland and GermanyThe home of workspaces opened in Europe, Africa and the Middle East. Backups stay inside the EU.

US

Oregon and VirginiaThe home of workspaces opened in the Americas and Asia-Pacific. Backups stay inside the US.

  • Every workspace’s data is isolated from every other’s, and production runs in its own accounts, apart from staging and development.
  • Least privilege: engineers have no standing access to production. Access is requested, approved, time-boxed and logged.
  • Infrastructure is written as code and reviewed like code. Nothing in production is changed by hand.

Application security

Every change to Crestora is read by a second engineer before it ships, and checked by machines before and after.

  • Code review on every change, with anything touching sign-in, sharing or billing also reviewed by our security team.
  • Dependency scanning on every build; critical fixes are patched within 72 hours.
  • Static analysis and secret scanning in the pipeline, so a key never reaches the repository.
  • An annual independent penetration test. The latest, in September 2026, found no critical or high-severity issues; the summary is available under NDA through Compliance.
  • Security training for everyone when they join, and again every year.

Incident response

If something goes wrong, we move fast and we tell you. The on-call crew works from a written playbook that we rehearse every quarter, and these are the promises we hold ourselves to for anything that touches your data.

  1. MinutesDetectAlerts page the on-call engineer, day or night, in every time zone.
  2. 1 hourContainWe stop the damage first: revoke, isolate, roll back.
  3. 72 hoursTell youAffected customers hear from a human, usually much sooner.
  4. 10 daysLearnA plain-language write-up of what happened and what we changed.

Safe harbour
  • Test only against your own workspace. Never access, change or keep anyone else’s data.
