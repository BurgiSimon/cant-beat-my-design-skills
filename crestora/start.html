The first ten minutes.

Every lane starts at Spark. Here’s how most people spend their first ten minutes, and none of it needs a manual.

  1. 01

    Catch the first spark

    Drop in a voice memo, a screenshot or one honest sentence. Sparkboard keeps it, and groups it with the next ones as they come.

    SparkboardAbout 2 minutes

    Shape it on one page
