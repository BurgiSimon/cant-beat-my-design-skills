Partner tiers

The road widens as you climb.

Every partner starts as Registered. Certification and the work you do with creators move you up, and each tier keeps everything from the one before.

Tier 01

Registered

Your first stretch of road. Apply, agree to the partner terms and you are in, usually within the week.

92 partners at this tier

How to get here

  1. Apply below and tell us what you build, run or teach.
  2. Agree to the partner terms. They fit on two pages.
  3. Join a 30-minute welcome call with the partner team.

What you get

  • The partner portal, with sandbox lanes and test webhooks
  • A Crew workspace for your own team, free for a year
  • Referral links that track every team you bring
  • A listing in the partner directory
  • The partner newsletter and early looks at what ships next

Tier 02

Certified

Proven with real teams. This is where most studio partners settle in, and where the referral share starts.

36 partners at this tier

How to get here

  1. Two people on your crew pass the Crestora certification (about six hours, free).
  2. Five teams are live on Crestora with your help.
  3. One short case study we can share, written with the client.

What you get

Everything in Registered, plus

  • The Certified badge and a higher place in the directory
  • 20% referral share for each team’s first year
  • Introductions to teams who ask us for a partner
  • A named partner manager
  • A Field Notes feature and a Launch Night slot

Tier 03

Premier

The whole road. A small group we build with closely, from the roadmap review to joint pitches for Label teams.

12 partners at this tier

How to get here

  1. Twenty-five or more teams live on Crestora with your help.
  2. A client satisfaction score above 4.6 out of 5.
  3. An invitation after a joint review with your partner manager.

What you get

Everything in Certified, plus

  • After the first year, 10% for as long as the team stays
  • Joint pitches for Label accounts
  • Early access and a seat at the roadmap review
  • The Partner Council, twice a year in Lisbon
  • A Premier listing on the integrations and partner pages
