Code of conduct

Four things we ask.

The crew stays kind because everyone in it keeps these. Moderators enforce them, gently first.

  1. 01

    Leave the light on

    Be kind, especially at 2 a.m. Assume the person on the other end is tired, trying and about to hit publish.

  2. 02

    Notes on the work, never the person

    Be specific, honest and generous. Say what works before what doesn’t, and say why.

  3. 03

    What’s shared in a lane stays there

    Unreleased work is someone’s livelihood. Don’t screenshot it, repost it or borrow from it.

  4. 04

    Show, don’t sell

    Share your work freely. Keep pitches, client hunting and affiliate links out of the forum and the Circles.

Something wrong? Use the flag on any post, or write to conduct@crestora.example. Fourteen volunteer moderators and two of our crew reply to every report within a day.
