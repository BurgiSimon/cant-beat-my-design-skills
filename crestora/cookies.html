The short version

Six lines, all of them true of this very page.

Cookies and local storage

In shortCookies travel to the server with every visit. Local storage stays in your browser. We sort both into three kinds.

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep and to send back with every request to that site. That’s how a site remembers you’re signed in from one page to the next.

Local storage is similar, but it stays in your browser: only the site’s own scripts can read it, and it’s never sent anywhere by itself. Session storage is the same thing with a shorter memory: it forgets everything when you close the tab.

Whatever we keep, we sort into three kinds:

Inside the Crestora app itself, where your lanes live, we use five cookies. All of them are first-party: set by Crestora and read only by Crestora.

Clearing it yourself

In shortYour browser can delete all of this without us. Nothing breaks; the site just forgets.

You don’t need our button. Every browser can remove a site’s data: look for cookies and site data in its settings and remove the entries for this site. A private window keeps nothing once you close it.

Clearing never breaks anything. The site simply forgets: Pricing goes back to yearly, your roadmap votes come undone, the API reference shows curl again, and the footer’s opening plays in full on your next page.
