FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Our team answers in under two minutes, day or night.

Is my money safe with AsterPay?

Your balance is held at regulated partner banks, kept separate from AsterPay's own funds, and protected by biometric sign-in, instant card freeze and round-the-clock fraud monitoring.

How much does AsterPay cost?

Standard is free: no monthly fee, free transfers between AsterPay users and a free virtual card. Plus and Metal add higher limits, cashback and the metal card.

How fast are transfers?

Payments to other AsterPay users arrive in under a second, any time. International transfers to bank accounts usually land within minutes and never take more than one business day.

Can I invest with a small amount?

Yes. Fractional shares let you buy into stocks and ETFs from $1, and auto-invest can put a fixed amount to work every week or month.

Which currencies can I hold?

You can hold and convert between 40+ currencies at the mid-market rate, and spend in 150+ with your card without foreign transaction fees.

How do I get started?

Download the app, verify your ID in about three minutes, and your virtual card is ready straight away. The physical card arrives within a week.
