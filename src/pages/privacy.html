01Who we are

Mist is built by Mist Labs, Inc. (“Mist”, “we”, “us”), a Delaware corporation founded in 2024. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or use the Mist platform (together, the “Service”).

It covers two kinds of information. For account and website data — like your name, email, and how you use the Service — we are the controller. For customer data — the content your workflows process — we act as a processor for the organization that owns the workspace, and handle it only on their instructions and under our Terms of service.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run and improve the Service.

  • Account information. Your name, work email, company, role, and password or single sign-on identity.
  • Customer data. The workflows you build and the data that flows through them: records from tools you connect, run inputs and outputs, files, and approval decisions.
  • Usage data. Which features you use, run counts and timings, and error reports, so we can keep the Service fast and reliable.
  • Device and log data. IP address, browser, and operating system, recorded in security and access logs.
  • Billing information. Your plan, billing contact, and invoices. Card details go straight to our payment processor — we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Communications. Messages you send to our support or sales teams, and your email preferences.

We don’t buy personal information from data brokers, and we don’t seek out sensitive categories of data. If your own workflows route that kind of data through Mist, it’s protected as customer data.

03How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, maintain, and secure the Service, including running your workflows;
  • authenticate you and protect accounts from fraud and abuse;
  • process payments and send receipts, trial reminders, and service notices;
  • answer support requests and, when you ask us to, help debug a workflow;
  • understand aggregate usage so we can improve features and performance;
  • comply with the law and enforce our Terms of service.

Under the GDPR, we rely on the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable service, your consent for optional emails (which you can withdraw at any time), and our legal obligations.

04AI and your data

AI steps — classify, extract, summarize, draft, and decide — are at the heart of Mist, so we hold them to a clear standard.

Your data is never used to train models. Not ours, and not our model providers’. This applies on every plan, including Starter, and to every AI step you run.

  • Hosted models. When you use Mist’s hosted models, we send only the inputs a step needs. Our model providers process them to return a result and are contractually prohibited from training on them or keeping them afterwards.
  • Bring your own key. If you connect your own model provider account, requests go to that provider under your agreement with them.
  • Outputs. AI outputs are customer data. They belong to you and are kept in your run history for your plan’s retention period.
  • Human review. Mist staff never read your workflow content unless you grant temporary access for support, or we must investigate a security incident or respond to a legal requirement.

05How we share information

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only:

  • with sub-processors that help us run the Service, under written agreements that require security and confidentiality at least as strict as ours;
  • with integrations you connect, when your workflows send data to them at your direction;
  • for legal reasons, in response to a valid legal request — we’ll tell you first unless the law prohibits it;
  • in a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, with this policy continuing to apply.

06Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it.

Account information
While your account is active, then deleted within 30 days of closing it.
Run history
7 days on Starter, 90 days on Team, and a period you set on Enterprise.
Encrypted backups
Rolled off automatically within 35 days.
Security and access logs
12 months.
Billing records
7 years, as tax and accounting law requires.

You can delete individual workflows and runs at any time. Deleted data leaves our live systems immediately and our backups within 35 days.

07Where we store data

Customer data is stored in the region chosen for your workspace: the United States or the European Union. Enterprise adds guaranteed data residency: customer data, backups, and AI processing all stay in that region.

When personal information moves between regions — for example, when a US-based support engineer helps a customer in the EU — we rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards.

08Security

Security is built into how Mist works, not added on later.

  • SOC 2 Type II, audited every year;
  • AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit;
  • SSO/SAML and SCIM provisioning, roles and permissions, and audit logs;
  • least-privilege staff access, protected by hardware security keys;
  • regular third-party penetration tests and a responsible disclosure program.

If a security incident affects your personal information, we’ll notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay — and within 72 hours where the GDPR requires it. For more detail, see our security overview.

09Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you, in a portable format;
  • correct information that’s inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

In the EU, EEA, and UK

These rights come from the GDPR and UK GDPR. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

In California

The CCPA gives you the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don’t sell or share personal information, so there’s nothing to opt out of — and we will never treat you differently for using your rights.

How to make a request

Most requests take a few clicks in Settings → Privacy. Otherwise, use our contact form. We verify your identity, respond within 30 days, and accept requests from authorized agents with your written permission. If your account belongs to your employer’s workspace, we’ll pass your request to them, since they control that data.

11Children

Mist is a tool for businesses and isn’t directed to children under 16. We don’t knowingly collect their personal information, and if we learn that we have, we delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy as Mist evolves. Before a material change takes effect, we’ll email workspace owners and show a notice in the app at least 30 days in advance. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest revision.
