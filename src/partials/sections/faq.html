01

What counts as a task?

A task is one action or AI step that runs — sending a message, updating a record, summarizing a document. Triggers, conditions, and retries are always free, so you only pay for work that actually gets done.

Starter includes 1,000 tasks a month. Team includes 25,000, then $8 per extra 1,000.

02

Is my data used to train AI models?

Never. Your workflows, data, and AI outputs are never used to train models — not ours, not anyone else’s. The model providers we work with are contractually barred from training on or storing what you send them.

03

Which AI models does Mist use?

Your choice. Mist-hosted models work out of the box with nothing to configure, or bring your own API key to use a provider you already trust. Pick the model for each step — a fast one to classify, a stronger one to draft — and swap it anytime without rebuilding the workflow.

04

Where is my data stored?

In the region you choose when you create your workspace: the EU or the US.

  • Encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit
  • SOC 2 Type II audited and GDPR compliant
  • Enterprise adds guaranteed data residency and custom retention
How we keep your data safe
05

What happens when my trial ends?

Your workspace moves to the free Starter plan automatically. There’s no card on file, so there’s nothing to cancel — and nothing is deleted. Workflows beyond Starter’s five active ones are paused, not removed, and pick up where they left off when you upgrade.

06

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from Billing settings whenever you like. Changes take effect immediately and are prorated to the day, so you only pay for what you use. Switching to annual billing saves 20%.

07

How does Mist handle failures?

Failed steps retry automatically with exponential backoff, so a flaky API rarely becomes your problem. If a run still can’t finish, Mist alerts you in chat or by email with the exact step and error. Fix the cause, then replay the run from history — nothing to rebuild, nothing lost.

08

Do you offer discounts for startups and nonprofits?

Yes — 50% off the Team plan for eligible early-stage startups and registered nonprofits. Contact sales with a few details about your organization, and we’ll apply the discount to your workspace.
