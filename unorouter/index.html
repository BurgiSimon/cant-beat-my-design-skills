    > faq / 6 entries
    
          
    Questions, answered.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            01Is it really the same price?
            
    Yes. Token prices match each provider’s own list price. We earn a small fee when you buy credits, never on the tokens themselves.
    
          
    
          
    
            02How are free models free?
            
    Providers offer them to let people try their models. They are rate-limited and may queue at peak times, but they use the same key and the same API as everything else.
    
          
    
          
    
            03Does it add latency?
            
    About 18 ms at the median, from edge routing in 14 regions. Streaming starts as soon as the upstream sends its first token.
    
          
    
          
    
            04Which SDKs work?
            
    Anything that speaks the OpenAI API: the official Python and TypeScript SDKs, LangChain, the Vercel AI SDK, LiteLLM, or plain HTTP.
    
          
    
          
    
            05What happens to my prompts?
            
    We don’t store prompts or completions by default. You can restrict a key to providers with zero data retention, per request or per key.
    
          
    
          
    
            06Can I bring my own provider keys?
            
    Yes. Add them in the dashboard and UnoRouter uses your rate limits and your discounts first, then falls back to ours.
